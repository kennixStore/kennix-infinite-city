# Project Decisions

A record of important decisions. **Agreed** means selected by the user; **Proposed** remains subject to discussion.

## Agreed direction
- Title: KENNIX: INFINITE CITY.
- Genre: open-world action, driving, racing and adventure.
- Browser-playable 3D game; PlayCanvas is the current engine.
- Multiple vehicle classes and customisation.
- Advanced vehicle damage is a target.
- Switchable third-person and first-person camera.
- Touch, phone tilt, keyboard and controller are desired control methods.
- Mix of real-world-inspired places and fictional cities.
- Start in one city and unlock more over time.
- Dynamic weather and environmental effects are long-term goals.
- Offline play, private/friends multiplayer and global competition are desired.
- Levels, XP, KX, achievements, events and rewards.
- KX is fictional game currency; players should earn, spend and transfer it.
- Vehicle theft and robbery are desired mechanics, subject to fair protections.
- Approximately 60-second opening cinematic.
- Current development environment: Android phone; no laptop.

## Proposed, not final
- Slogan: "One City. Endless Possibilities."
- First city name: NOVA CITY.
- District names/layout, story plot, supporting characters and blackout opening.
- Final logo styling and palette.
- Rules for interactions involving other online players.
- Rewarded-ad provider and monetisation rates.

## Technical guardrails
1. Keep this repository separate from KENNIX Store.
2. Never trust client-side currency or race results.
3. Do not claim a feature is finished until integrated and tested.
4. Test on actual Android hardware.
5. Use original or properly licensed assets; do not copy another game's protected content.
