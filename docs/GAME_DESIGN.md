# Game Design — KENNIX: INFINITE CITY

## Identity
An original browser-first 3D open-world action, driving, racing and adventure game. Visual target: cinematic city environments and detailed vehicles. Broad genre inspiration is acceptable; copied maps, characters, logos, artwork, missions, music, code or distinctive sequences are not.

## Agreed gameplay direction
- Open-world exploration, endless racing and mission-based racing.
- Cars, SUVs, supercars, motorcycles, trucks and additional vehicle classes.
- Customisation: paint, rims, spoilers, neon, engine, nitro and performance tyres.
- Advanced damage: visible damage, broken lights, crash consequences and garage repairs.
- Vehicle theft and robbery, with fair protections for online play.
- Customisable player character.
- Third-person and first-person/cockpit camera.
- Touch, tilt, keyboard and external controller controls.
- Engine, tyre, horn, crash, nitro, environment and music audio.
- KX currency earned, spent and transferred between players.
- Optional rewarded ads and purchases; no forced interruptions during normal gameplay.

## World and progression
- Fictional and real-world-inspired locations.
- Unlockable cities, with the first city freely explorable.
- Dynamic weather, day/night and environmental effects.
- Levels, XP, achievements, events, challenges, rankings and optional VIP features.
- Offline, friends/private rooms and global competitions.

## Online fair-play requirements
- Clear ownership and recovery rules for vehicles.
- Anti-harassment protections and limits on repeated targeting.
- Auditable KX transfers and abuse detection.
- Server-side validation of rewards, balances and competitive results.
- Report/block tools and moderation.
- No real-money cash-out without separate legal, financial and platform review.

## Opening cinematic
Target length approximately 60 seconds, combining a continuous camera journey, dramatic action and character moments. A citywide blackout is one proposal, not a locked sequence. The intro must be original.

## Unconfirmed proposals
NOVA CITY, district names, the blackout storyline, characters, exact missions, police behaviour, properties, online theft rules, final logo, slogan and monetisation values all require confirmation before being treated as final.
